About Us

Operators, not just advisors.

SentinelSec is a boutique consultancy built by former SOC leads, cloud architects, and incident commanders. We sit with your team until controls actually work in production — not just on a slide.

How we show up

We partner with mid-market and enterprise organizations that need a clear picture of risk, a practical roadmap, and hands-on help implementing it. No black-box reports. No theater.

Engagements typically start with a focused discovery workshop, then move into assessment or architecture work with weekly demos of progress — so stakeholders see risk drop in real time, not after a 12-week silence.

  • Mapped to NIST CSF, ISO 27001, and CIS Controls
  • Board-ready reporting without the jargon fog
  • Named senior practitioners on every engagement
  • Knowledge transfer built into every deliverable
