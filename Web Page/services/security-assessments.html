Service detail

Security assessments

Gap analysis against your regulatory overlay, with a prioritized 90-day plan your engineers can execute.

Why this engagement

Stop guessing which controls matter. We map your current state to the frameworks you must satisfy, score residual risk, and hand your team a sequenced backlog they can actually ship.

Who it is for

  • Security and IT leaders preparing for audit or board review
  • Teams inheriting an unknown environment after M&A
  • Organizations stuck between frameworks with conflicting advice

What we cover

  • Control gap analysis against NIST CSF, ISO 27001, CIS Controls, or your sector overlay (HIPAA, PCI DSS, SOC 2)
  • Asset, identity, and data-flow discovery workshops
  • Policy and evidence sampling — not a checkbox theater review
  • Risk-ranked findings with business impact language for executives
  • A 30 / 60 / 90-day remediation plan with owners and effort bands

How we work

  1. Week 1: Kickoff, document intake, and stakeholder interviews
  2. Weeks 2–3: Technical and process assessment across people, process, and tech
  3. Week 4: Findings workshop, roadmap agreement, and final report

What you receive

  • Executive summary and board-ready slides
  • Detailed control matrix with evidence notes
  • Prioritized 90-day engineering plan
  • Optional re-check after the first sprint of remediations
