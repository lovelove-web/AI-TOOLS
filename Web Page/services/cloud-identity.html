Cloud & identity

AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud hardening, plus zero-trust identity design that people will actually use.

Why this engagement

Most cloud breaches are identity and configuration problems. We harden accounts, reduce standing privilege, and design access paths your workforce can follow without shadow IT.

Who it is for

  • Teams migrating to or expanding in AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud
  • Organizations fighting MFA gaps and over-privileged admins
  • Companies ready for zero-trust but unsure where to start

What we cover

  • Landing-zone and account structure review
  • IAM / Entra ID / Google Workspace privilege analysis
  • Conditional access, MFA, and session controls
  • Network segmentation, private connectivity, and logging baselines
  • Secrets, keys, and workload identity recommendations

How we work

  1. Discover: Read-only access, architecture interviews, and identity inventory
  2. Harden: Prioritized fix list plus reference configurations
  3. Enable: Workshops with platform and identity owners to adopt the new model

What you receive

  • Cloud & identity risk scorecard
  • Target-state architecture diagrams
  • Privilege reduction backlog
  • Runbook snippets for common admin workflows
