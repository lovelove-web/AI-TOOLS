vCISO & program build

Fractional CISO leadership, board reporting, and a security program that scales with headcount.

Why this engagement

You may not need a full-time CISO yet — but you do need strategy, prioritization, and someone who can speak board and engineering. Our vCISO service fills that seat with senior judgment.

Who it is for

  • Growth-stage companies preparing for SOC 2 or enterprise customers
  • Organizations between CISOs or building a program from scratch
  • Boards that want clearer risk reporting without a bloated GRC tool

What we cover

  • Security strategy and annual roadmap
  • Board and audit committee reporting
  • Vendor risk and security questionnaire support
  • Hiring plans and mentoring for internal security hires
  • Program metrics that track risk reduction, not vanity KPIs

How we work

  1. Month 1: Baseline assessment and 90-day operating plan
  2. Ongoing: Weekly or bi-weekly cadence with leadership and engineering
  3. Quarterly: Board pack, roadmap refresh, and budget guidance

What you receive

  • Security program charter and RACI
  • Quarterly board risk report
  • Policy starter set tailored to your stack
  • Vendor and customer security FAQ pack

Tell us your stack and timeline. We will recommend a scoped proposal.
