Awareness & training

Role-based workshops for executives, developers, and helpdesk — measured by behavior change, not click rates alone.

Why this engagement

Generic phishing videos rarely change behavior. We design short, role-specific learning that matches how people actually work — then measure what improved.

Who it is for

  • Companies tired of annual click-through compliance courses
  • Engineering orgs that want secure-coding habits
  • Executive teams that need decision drills, not jargon

What we cover

  • Role-based curricula for executives, developers, IT, and helpdesk
  • Phishing simulation programs with coaching, not shame
  • Secure coding workshops tied to your languages and pipelines
  • Executive tabletop-style decision sessions
  • Metrics: report rates, time-to-report, and policy adherence

How we work

  1. Design: Audience map and baseline behavior metrics
  2. Deliver: Live workshops plus micro-learning reinforcement
  3. Measure: Quarterly scorecard and content refresh

What you receive

  • Curriculum map by role
  • Workshop decks and facilitator notes
  • Phishing program configuration guidance
  • Quarterly awareness scorecard
