Service detail

Incident response

Retainer coverage, forensics coordination, and tabletop drills so the first real incident is not your first rehearsal.

Why this engagement

When something breaks at 2 a.m., you need a calm voice and a clear plan. We help you prepare before that night — and stand with you if it arrives.

Who it is for

  • Organizations without a mature SOC or IR retainer
  • Teams that have never run a realistic tabletop
  • Leaders who need forensics coordination without hiring a full-time team

What we cover

  • IR retainer with defined response SLAs
  • Playbook development for ransomware, BEC, and insider scenarios
  • Tabletop facilitation for execs, IT, legal, and communications
  • Forensics partner coordination and evidence handling guidance
  • Post-incident reviews and lasting control improvements

How we work

  1. Prepare: Assess readiness, build playbooks, and schedule drills
  2. Respond: Activate retainer, contain, and communicate with stakeholders
  3. Improve: After-action report and control upgrades

What you receive

  • Role-based IR playbooks
  • Contact trees and decision checklists
  • Tabletop after-action report
  • Retainer onboarding pack for your helpdesk and on-call teams
