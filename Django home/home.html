Our Story

A kitchen built by five chefs, one dining room.

La Maison started as a single French bistro. Today five chefs — from Nairobi, Bangkok, Oaxaca, Helsinki and Lyon — share one kitchen, so you can order a plate of jollof rice and a Karelian pie on the same ticket.

Every recipe stays true to its region: real tamarind, real reindeer, real sumac and saffron, sourced from the communities that inspired it.
